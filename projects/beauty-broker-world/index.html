Project case study / Healthcare & Beauty Technology

Production SaaS

Beauty Broker
World.

A SaaS platform supporting healthcare and beauty professionals with tools for appointments, treatment catalogs, subscriptions, payments, messaging, and administration.

Explore the case study ↓

01 / Overview

Overview

Beauty Broker World is a production SaaS product for the healthcare and beauty sector. It brings recurring operational workflows into one web platform, helping professionals and organizations coordinate appointments, services, payments, and day-to-day administration.

The product is designed around the needs of multiple user roles and the practical work involved in running service-based businesses.

02 / Contribution

My Role

I am responsible for end-to-end product development, working across the frontend and backend. My contribution includes building responsive interfaces, developing application functionality, connecting services, and supporting the product through deployment and ongoing maintenance.

I also work on authentication and role-based access, relational data, and payment integrations as part of the product experience.

03 / Technology

Tech Stack

The product uses a modern web stack for application development, data, payments, and deployment.

  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Stripe Connect
  • Docker
  • Redis
  • Vercel

04 / Product

Product Capabilities

At a high level, the platform supports workflows such as:

  • Multi-role SaaS platform
  • Appointment management
  • Treatment catalogs
  • Subscription management
  • Payment integrations
  • Messaging workflows
  • Administrative tools

05 / Practice

Engineering Approach

I approach SaaS product development by keeping functionality modular, making core workflows responsive, and integrating external services in ways that fit the user experience. I favor readable, maintainable code and incremental delivery so a product can be improved as its needs change.

This case study describes my role and the product at a general level. It intentionally excludes internal screens and implementation details.
